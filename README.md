# 🚦 TrafficOps+  
### Safer, Greener City Traffic Playbook  

---

## 📌 Overview  
TrafficOps+ is a **computer vision–powered, plug-and-play traffic management kit** that redefines “smart traffic” by going beyond just vehicle flow.  
We focus on **emergency response, pedestrian safety, and pollution reduction**, creating safer and greener cities.  

---

## ❌ Problem Statement  
Current "smart traffic" solutions are **vehicle-centric** and miss out on real city pain-points:  
- 🚑 **Emergency vehicles** stuck in congestion → costing lives.  
- 🚶 **Unsafe pedestrian crossings** & ignored vulnerable road users (cyclists, rickshaws, e-bikes).  
- 🌍 **Pollution hotspots** near hospitals/schools due to long vehicle idling.  

---

## ✅ Our Solution  
TrafficOps+ introduces an **AI-driven traffic playbook**:  
- 🔊 **Emergency detection** → Identify ambulances/fire trucks & auto-suggest **green-wave routes**.  
- 🚶 **Pedestrian-first approach** → Detect pedestrians/non-motorized vehicles & dynamically adapt crossing times.  
- 🌱 **Eco-aware signals** → Link congestion to pollution data → Recommend short-cycle adjustments near sensitive areas.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- **Computer Vision** → YOLOv8, OpenCV  
- **Programming** → Python  
- **Visualization & Dashboard** → Streamlit / Flask + Plotly  
- **Data Source** → YouTube traffic/crowd videos (MVP)  

---

## 🎯 Hackathon MVP  
- **Input** → Recorded traffic videos  
- **Processing** → Object detection & event-triggered logic  
- **Output (Dashboard)** →  
  - 🚑 Ambulance detected → Extend green light by **+30s** at Lane 2  
  - 🚶 Pedestrian surge → Trigger crosswalk phase  
  - 🏥 Hospital zone congestion → Cut idling by **20%**  
- **Visuals** → Bounding boxes + KPIs (**Lives saved, Emissions reduced, Pedestrian risk lowered**)  

---

## 📊 Sample Workflow  
1. Feed traffic video into system  
2. YOLOv8 + OpenCV detect objects (ambulance, pedestrian, car, bike, etc.)  
3. Logic engine triggers **signal adjustments**  
4. Dashboard displays video overlay + real-time KPIs  

---

## 🌍 Future Scope  
- 🔗 Integration with **real-time traffic lights** via IoT/Edge devices  
- 🚑 **City-wide emergency routing** system  
- 📡 Expansion to **multi-city deployments**  
- 📊 Policy-level data integration for **greener & safer traffic planning**
